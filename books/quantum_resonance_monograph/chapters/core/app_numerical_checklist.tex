\section{Reproducibility checklist for every representation}
\label{app:numerical-checklist}

For a complex-scaling calculation, the following information is sufficient to
reconstruct most systematic choices:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the coordinate contour, scaling angle, and analytic branches of every
  multivalued function;

  \item the basis functions, nonlinear scales, truncation size, quadrature,
  and overlap-matrix cutoff;

  \item right and left residuals, matrix condition estimates, and the
  normalization convention;

  \item eigenvalue trajectories under changes of $\theta$, basis family,
  nonlinear scales, and precision;

  \item the reference Hamiltonian and matched discretization used for a CLD;

  \item an independent benchmark for representative poles and, when possible,
  residues;

  \item the square-root branch used to convert $E$ to $\omega$ in a
  black-hole problem.
\end{enumerate}

For an exact-WKB calculation, report the spectral curve, branch cuts, Stokes
graph, Borel direction, lateral prescription, integration cycles, truncation
order, Borel--Pad\'e order, and stability under changes of resummation
parameters.  If a monodromy counterterm is used, report the regulator, matching
condition, and scheme-dependent finite parts.

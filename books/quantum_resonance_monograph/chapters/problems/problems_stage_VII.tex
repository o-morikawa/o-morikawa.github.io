\section{Problems for Stage VII}
\label{problems:stage-VII}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{VII.\arabic*},leftmargin=*]
  \item Diagonalize a generic $2\times2$ complex-symmetric matrix and derive
  the discriminant condition for a second-order exceptional point.

  \item Construct a Jordan chain and derive the double-pole and simple-pole
  terms of its resolvent.  Compare with a double zero of
  $\Cincoming(E)$.

  \item Prove self-orthogonality of the coalesced right vector with its left
  partner by differentiating the eigenvalue equation.

  \item Encircle the square-root branch point once and twice.  Track
  eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and all gauge choices needed to compare the final
  vector with the initial one.

  \item Calculate the biorthogonal Berry connection for the $2\times2$ model.
  Determine which part changes under rescaling of left and right vectors.

  \item Replace a delta-normalized continuum state by a momentum bin of width
  $\Delta k$.  Find the divergent normalization as $\Delta k\to0$ and isolate
  a finite holonomy.

  \item Derive an RG-type equation that enforces bin-width independence of a
  renormalized geometric phase.  Identify its integration constant as a
  physical convention or datum.

  \item Investigate an EP approaching a continuum threshold.  Explain how the
  square-root branch from the EP competes with the threshold branch of the
  resolvent.

  \item Compute eigenvalue condition numbers around a loop enclosing an EP.
  Relate their peaks to pseudospectral sensitivity.

  \item Formulate a multiple-zero search using
  $\mathcal C=\partial_E\mathcal C=0$.  Propose a numerical continuation test
  that distinguishes an EP from an accidental near crossing.
\end{enumerate}

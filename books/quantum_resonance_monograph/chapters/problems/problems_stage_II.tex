\section{Problems for Stage II}
\label{problems:stage-II}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{II.\arabic*},leftmargin=*]
  \item Derive the reduced mass and center-of-mass separation for two
  particles.  State precisely which potential hypotheses allow the internal
  and center-of-mass domains to factorize.

  \item Starting from the resolvent identity, derive the Lippmann--Schwinger
  equation for $\ket{\psi^{(+)}}$.  Check the sign of the outgoing Green
  function using the time convention of the book.

  \item Evaluate the free Green kernel in three dimensions and recover its
  outgoing asymptotic form.  Use it to derive the first Born amplitude.

  \item Derive the probability current and the factor $k_f/k_i$ in a
  multichannel differential cross section.  Show how flux normalization
  removes this factor from the unitarity relation for the on-shell $S$ matrix.

  \item Prove the optical theorem from partial-wave unitarity.  Then insert a
  complex optical potential and identify the additional reaction cross
  section.

  \item Express a radial Green function in terms of regular and outgoing Jost
  solutions.  Show that a zero of the Jost function gives a pole and compute
  the residue for a simple zero.

  \item Derive the logarithmic Coulomb phase by integrating the potential
  along a straight asymptotic trajectory.  Explain why replacing a Coulomb
  wave by a plane wave changes the definition of the scattering amplitude.

  \item Verify directly that the ordinary M{\o}ller limit fails for the
  Coulomb Hamiltonian at the level of the phase.  Construct the corresponding
  Dollard modifier in the one-particle relative problem.

  \item List the three cluster asymptotic regions of a three-charge problem
  and the different large variables in each.  Explain why pairwise Coulomb
  phases do not automatically give a uniform approximation in their overlap.

  \item For a coherent soft-photon dressing with profile $f(\bm k)$, determine
  the condition for a finite Fock norm.  Compare it with the infrared behavior
  required by the Coulomb field of a charged particle.

  \item Derive Lagrange's identity
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:lagrange-identity} for the general Sturm--Liouville
  expression.  Show that two functions obeying the same separated regular-
  endpoint condition have zero boundary bracket.

  \item For $-\rmd^2/\rmd r^2+g/r^2$, calculate both Frobenius exponents and
  reproduce the limit-point threshold $g=3/4$.  State the domain condition
  needed for the physical $s$ wave.

  \item Derive the sign and normalization of the Green kernel
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:sl-green-kernel} by integrating across $r=r'$.  Repeat for
  the opposite resolvent convention.

  \item Match the regular square-well solution at $r=a$ and obtain
  Eqs.~\eqref{eq:square-well-jost-function} and
  \eqref{eq:square-well-resonance-condition}.  Track one bound-state zero as
  the well depth is reduced through threshold.

  \item Differentiate the radial equation with respect to $k$ and derive
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:wronskian-derivative-integral}.  Evaluate the boundary term
  for a decaying bound state and explain which analytic continuation is
  required for a Gamow state.
\end{enumerate}

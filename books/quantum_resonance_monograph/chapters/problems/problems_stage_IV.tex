\section{Problems for Stage IV}
\label{problems:stage-IV}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{IV.\arabic*},leftmargin=*]
  \item For a rank-one $P$ space, evaluate the Feshbach self-energy for a
  flat continuum with a finite threshold.  Separate its principal-value shift
  and imaginary width.

  \item Derive the action of a real dilation on $x$, $p$, and the kinetic
  energy.  Analytically continue the parameter and obtain
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:H-theta}.

  \item For an outgoing wave $\rme^{\rmi kr}$ with $\im k<0$, derive the range
  of scaling angles for which it becomes square integrable.  Translate the
  result to the location of the rotated energy cut.

  \item Prove the free essential-spectrum rotation directly in momentum
  space.  Then use relative compactness to explain the corresponding step in
  the ABC theorem.

  \item Carry out Berggren's Gaussian regularization for
  $u(r)\sim\rme^{\rmi kr}$.  Evaluate the integral first where it converges and
  then analytically continue to the resonance quadrant.

  \item Choose a Schwartz test space and show how a Gamow functional acts on
  it after contour deformation.  Identify which continuity estimate would
  fail on the full Hilbert norm topology.

  \item Derive the continuum level density from the trace of a resolvent
  difference.  Show how its integral is related to a scattering phase under
  trace-class hypotheses.

  \item Diagonalize a complex-scaled finite basis at three values of $\theta$.
  Give an algorithm that classifies bound states, stable resonances, and
  rotated-continuum pseudostates without using their colors in a plot.

  \item For a complex-symmetric matrix, derive the $c$ product and compare it
  with ordinary biorthogonality.  Give an example in which using Hermitian
  conjugation produces the wrong pole residue.

  \item Explain, with a contour diagram, why Zel'dovich damping, complex
  rotation, and a rigged-space pairing can agree on a resonance norm even
  though none is an ordinary $L^2(\real)$ norm.

  \item For $D(z)=z-E_d-\Sigma(z)$, expand about a simple complex zero and
  derive $Z_{\mathrm R}=[1-\Sigma'(z_{\mathrm R})]^{-1}$.  Give an example
  showing that $Z_{\mathrm R}$ need not be real or lie in $[0,1]$.

  \item Starting from the unitary pole factor
  Eq.~\eqref{eq:unitary-pole-factor}, derive the Lorentzian phase derivative
  and the peak time delay in Eq.~\eqref{eq:breit-wigner-time-delay}.

  \item Add a constant coherent background to a simple pole and derive the
  Fano form.  Determine the energy of the maximum and zero for real $q$, and
  show how an imaginary part of $q$ fills the zero.

  \item Let a channel width obey $\Gamma_l(E)=\gamma k^{2l+1}$.  Compare the
  complex pole, the maximum of $|D(E)|^{-2}$, and the half-maximum width as
  the pole approaches threshold.
\end{enumerate}
